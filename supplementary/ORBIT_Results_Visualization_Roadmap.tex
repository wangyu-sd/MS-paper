\paragraph{Generation-curve reproducibility.}
The cumulative full-test development data are in
\texttt{figures/data/gen\_curve\_main.csv}, while the
separately evaluated CAL200 episode/promotion data are in
\texttt{figures/data/gen\_curve\_rsi.csv}.
The former uses actual archived generation intervals; the latter
uses five individually evaluated episodes. H$_1$/H$_2$ is
presented as an additional isomer-ranking analysis rather than an
unlabelled extra learning generation.

\paragraph{Independent chemical evidence.}
Measured spectrum data, original molecular structures, simulated fragment
predictions and model-bounded isomer resolution are each retained with
their source provenance. Independent standards or independent-source
spectra enter the final evidence panels when those actual results are
available; the ongoing manuscript preserves their designated slots.
