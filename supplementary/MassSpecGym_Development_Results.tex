\section{Completed MassSpecGym development-set experiments}
\label{sec:msdev_results}

All results in this section were obtained on an \emph{adaptive development partition derived from the official MassSpecGym TRAIN fold}, not on the official VAL or TEST folds. The 8,401-query SEARCH\_DEV cohort was repeatedly inspected during development and is not a pristine independent test. Case-weighted scores count all eligible spectra, including failures; molecule-group bootstrap intervals account for repeated acquisitions of the same molecular connectivity. For de novo structure experiments, the reference precursor formula is hidden unless explicitly stated; equality uses the first 14 InChIKey characters (constitutional connectivity rather than stereochemistry). Retrieval Hit@$k$, de novo Exact@$k$, formula recall, fragment-mass support and conditional V recall describe \emph{different outcomes} and must not be compared as if they were the same metric.
