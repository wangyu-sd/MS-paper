\label{sec:msdev_denovo_literature}

Table~\ref{tab:msdev_denovo_external} juxtaposes literature-reported de novo
MassSpecGym results with molecular pretraining and the external resources
used for spectral conditioning and inference-time initialization. We distinguish
the \emph{known-formula} bonus setting, in which the reference molecular formula
is supplied, from the \emph{predicted-formula} setting. These are separate tasks.
Counts of unique molecules, molecular records, and sampled graph-edit examples
are not interchangeable. The numbers below were transcribed from the source
publications and were not reproduced under a common molecular pretraining
corpus, generation budget, ranking procedure, or MCES implementation.

\noindent\textbf{Data provenance and comparison limitations.}
The known-formula MADGEN, MS-BART, DiffMS, FOAM and FRIGID results
were collected or audited by MassSpecGym v1.5
\citep{Liu2026MassSpecGymWild}; MBGen, MS-GPT and GEMS are
reported according to their respective studies
\citep{Sun2026MBGen,Zhao2026MSGPT,Acikalin2026GEMS}.
Predicted-formula results use the MIST-CF top-five setting as reported
for DiffMS and FRIGID \citep{Bohde2025,Bohde2026FRIGID}.
MS-BART uses approximately 4 million molecular fingerprint--structure
pairs based on MassSpecGym's MCES2-disjoint corpus
\citep{Han2025MSBART}; DiffMS and MBGen use approximately 2.8 million
molecular structures from DSSTox, HMDB, COCONUT and MOSES
\citep{Bohde2025,Sun2026MBGen}. FRIGID uses SAFE-derived molecular
pretraining at a scale up to approximately one billion records, whereas
MS-GPT reuses SAFE-GPT initialization and approximately 100 million
molecular records \citep{Bohde2026FRIGID,Zhao2026MSGPT}. GEMS
samples 125 million \emph{graph-edit training examples} from PubChem
isomer pairs; the 125-million figure is not the number of distinct
molecules. GEMS uses formula-matched PubChem inference starts, with
dedicated restricted-start controls \citep{Acikalin2026GEMS}.
FOAM also employs PubChem seeds and ICEBERG-based scoring
\citep{Manjrekar2026FOAM}. Such seed-assisted searches do not represent
the same inference contract as generation without external starting
structures. The Top-10 Tanimoto for MBGen (0.47) is taken from its
primary report \citep{Sun2026MBGen}. The GEMS row for MS-GPT, when
reproduced by its authors, is \emph{not} substituted for the original
MS-GPT publication. MCES implementations and candidate budgets are
not fully harmonized, particularly for MS-GPT; the historical
inflated MIST--MolForge results identified in the MassSpecGym v1.5
audit are excluded \citep{Liu2026MassSpecGymWild}.

\noindent\textbf{ORBIT-MS reporting boundary.}
The rows above concern external published methods only. The available
ORBIT-MS PR93 results comprise an adaptively reused development sample
and an incomplete official TEST run, neither of which supports a final
held-out MassSpecGym de novo leaderboard entry. An ORBIT-MS entry
requires a frozen checkpoint, specified molecular pretraining and
inference-time seed resources, a consistent ranking budget, and
complete independently evaluated predictions for all six metrics.
