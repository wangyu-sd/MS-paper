# ORBIT-MS Nature discovery-first — six-figure experimental evidence plan

**Controlling story:** MS-paper PR #2 original Nature discovery-first Figure Plan restored in PR #7. The manuscript is organized by *scientific discovery*, not by algorithm sections: **Fig. 1 discover families → Fig. 2 establish chemical relationships → Fig. 3 validate structural reliability → Fig. 4 show reproducible sample-context biology → Fig. 5 test perturbations → Fig. 6 confirm selected chemistry**. The execution dependency order can differ from narrative order.

**Paper title:** Accurate molecular structure elucidation from tandem mass spectra with ORBIT-MS (target; "Accurate" must be supported by official complete held-out validation).

**Scientific-analysis rule:** No sampled/pilot cohort is a substitute for the complete declared eligible population. Streaming, deterministic sharding, indexed retrieval and caching are allowed to finish the full analysis. Preserve failures, inaccessible data, nulls and negative results.

Model/chemical-confidence baseline (needed for Fig. 1b,c; Fig. 2; Fig. 3).** Freeze the final source-bounded G/W/V checkpoint and input contract from orbit-ms PR93 or its validated successor; preserve TRAIN/promotion/development group disjointness and full official test sealing. Compare G-only, G+terminal W/V on identical candidates, and G+online W/V+terminal under the same budget. Separately report conditional World fragment support and V verification as supplied-precursor experiments; do not promote them as molecule accuracy. Implement independent candidate-set truth containment, risk–coverage, bounded isomer/core/formula reporting and calibration. No TEST label may be used to train or select a successor.

**2. Empirical public-resource boundary (needed for Fig. 1a and 1d).** Execute the complete-index-source orbit-ms PR94 census across GNPS/MassIVE, MetaboLights, Metabolomics Workbench, NORMAN/DSFP, DDBJ MetaboBank and MB-POST. Reconcile study accession, heterogeneous file records, physically verified MS/MS acquisition runs, raw scan counts, reference-assessable biological scans and library outcomes. A published StructureMASST-scale reference is not a newly computed project population. Keep blocked/incomplete source records visible, prove cross-repository non-duplication or avoid summed totals. Full source-bound three-study results already exist but are not globally representative: 348 expected / 272 verified / 76 unavailable files; 835,953 biological queries; 613,331 no accepted reference match; 2,255 recurrent *spectral communities*, 98 observed in all three studies. The particular degree-preserving three-study conditional topology null was **not enriched (p=0.91)**. Spectral communities do not constitute structure-supported new metabolite families. Full eligible recurrence and blanks/technical nulls must remain complete and source-aware.

**3. Chemical family identity (needed for dominant Fig. 1c and Fig. 2).** Apply frozen independently calibrated final ORBIT-MS to the complete accepted public-data study population, keeping out-of-support and unresolved outputs. Freeze family chemical membership **before** independent study replication, biological labels and novelty knowledge revelation. Collapse different adducts/charge states/redundant acquisitions only after source-verifiable grouping and over/under-merge control. Build family-level one-record-per-family novelty distance × independent-study recurrence result with no post hoc tuning; compare study-aware nulls and validate family structures. Do not relabel structural family counts from COSINE spectral clusters or uncalibrated Top1 graphs. For Fig. 2, generate real RDKit 2D relationships and reproducible graph-edit definitions, graph-neighbour distance, seed-prior- and chemistry-matched nulls, cross-class edit recurrence and genuine dated reference additions using T0-only knowledge. The available output may be negative or small; never invent family novelty to fill dominant panels.

**4. Biological distribution (needed for Fig. 4).** Independently curate actual public biological study/sample metadata with organism, sample context, platform, assay and shared-sample safeguards; complete eligibility table, biological sample counts and exclusions. Freeze structurally supported molecular family IDs before touching phenotype annotations. Use within-study adjusted detection/enrichment effects rather than pooling incompatible raw intensities. Full family × sample-context distributions, independent heldout-cohort effects, cross-lab sign replication, label permutations, known-metabolite references, batch/instrument/source-missingness sensitivity; report all eligible contexts including no-effect families. Fig. 4b remains the dominant display if the evidence is sufficient.

**5. Perturbation/utility (needed for Fig. 5).** Complete accessible verified germ-free, antibiotic, diet and other intervention cohorts, including exact sample IDs and time/covariate/contrast metadata. Recover known-metabolite positive-control effects under the identical analysis and run phenotype-permutation nulls. Report frozen family × intervention effect size with confidence intervals and corrected multiplicity on every eligible controlled comparison. Evaluate cross-intervention heterogeneity, failed replication and biological-process qualifiers. Compare anonymous features and supported frozen family grouping in the exact same biological samples/covariates using a preregistered primary endpoint. Do not claim microbial biosynthesis or biological causality without additional direct evidence. Fig. 5c is the dominant graph.

**6. Orthogonal confirmation (needed for Fig. 6).** Freeze full list and prioritization rules for family anchors and accessible independent reference/standards before seeing outcomes. Record raw sample precursor/MS/MS, authenticated independent standard peaks and collision/adduct conditions, actual comparable RT/coelution if measured, optional actual MSn/CCS when available; failures and ambiguous alternatives must stay in denominator. Independent chemical graph relationship and novelty audit must distinguish new-to-library from previously unrecognized family relationships or genuinely previously unreported structures. Retest biological claims only on verified structures without cherry-picking confirmed cases. A single decisive validated case is a desirable target, not a promised result.
