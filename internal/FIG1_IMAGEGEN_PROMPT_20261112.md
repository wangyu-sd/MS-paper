## 1. Figure 1 is a scientifically explanatory method figure

The paper contributes (i) **MMA** (measured peak → candidate structural motifs → alignment with a growing molecular graph), (ii) **G** (Peak-Ladder / Canonical Hybrid atom/ring/motif precursor graph construction), (iii) **W/V** (spectrum-blind candidate fragmentation followed by measured-spectrum discrimination), and (iv) **Scientific Improver RSI** (failure-derived, experimentally measured WGV/knowledge updates with episodic decision memory). The final objective is **new recoverable molecular structures in unannotated experimental MS/MS**.

The figure must communicate the three real bottlenecks:
- **Coverage:** the correct precursor graph may never be generated from an incomplete spectrum.
- **Discrimination:** even if generated, a same-formula incorrect structure can outrank it because many peaks are shared.
- **Learning:** a static model cannot decide which specific missing chemical capability to improve after repeated failures.

**Critical implementation boundaries:** The unknown spectrum and its observed fragment peaks are inputs to G/MMA and V; **not to W**. Target ground-truth structures are used only in labelled TRAIN FIT diagnostics, never fed into inference on unannotated unknown spectra. PR96 currently supports four **fixed types** of scientific action and recent episodic history; chemistry-specific action targets / semantic experience retrieval / cost-aware choice are **research proposals**, not implemented outcomes. The initial controlled RSI keeps W fixed and does not fine-tune the Agent LLM weights.

## 4. MASTER TEXT-TO-IMAGE PROMPT (copy this entire block)

> Create a **publication-quality scientific method Figure 1** for a Nature Machine Intelligence study titled “ORBIT-MS: Self-improving molecular structure elucidation of metabolomic dark matter”. It must visually explain a nontrivial scientific solution, not just show a sequence of neural-network boxes.
>
> **Scientific problem:** A real experimental MS/MS spectrum provides fragment-ion masses and intensities, but several different molecular connectivities can share formula and fragment evidence. Structural inference has two distinct obstacles: producing the true molecule among chemically valid candidates, and distinguishing it from near-isomeric incorrect candidates. A third obstacle is the repeated failure of a static model to learn which molecular chemistry it lacks. ORBIT-MS links peak-conditioned graph motifs (MMA), constrained molecular generation (G), forward fragmentation and spectral verification (W/V), and science-directed learning from failures (Scientific Improver Agent).
>
> **Editorial style:** 16:9 horizontal page, pure white background, sharp original vector-like scientific design. Precisely aligned panel letters a–e, thin analytical axes, modest annotation text, no decorative images. Color roles: observed chemical measurements charcoal #283239; candidate structures slate/blue-gray #637C8B; motif alignment and discriminating evidence low-saturation teal #4E8883; unresolved alternatives warm light gray #BEC5C5. Black and gray chemistry bonds, readable heteroatoms, large enough molecule/spectrum spaces to remain legible at 180 mm article width. No gradients, glowing neural brains, random molecule pictograms, 3D spheres, rounded dashboard cards, oversized arrow ribbons or generic agent icons.
>
> **Three spatial levels:** very narrow upper strip (15%) establishing the *chemical ambiguity*; wide middle region (55%) containing the *actual mechanism from peak through chemical motifs and molecular candidates to evidence*; lower strip (30%) containing *specific scientific feedback*. The upper section is panel a. In the middle, use three asymmetrical contiguous work areas b (MMA, 31%), c (G, 29%), d (W and V, 40%). The lower section is one connected panel e for agent-directed RSI. Emphasize MMA and structurally meaningful fragment evidence rather than acronym-box architecture.
>
> **Panel a — WHY STRUCTURE ELUCIDATION IS DIFFICULT.** Place one authentic measured MS/MS stick-spectrum insertion area with precursor/adduct metadata and a few highlighted observed peaks. To its right are two same-elemental-composition but different-bond-connectivity precursor graph insertion areas. A small headline reads: “One observed spectrum; several compatible molecular graphs”. The real spectrum and exact 2D graphs will come from one source-verified ORBIT-MS benchmark or unknown-source case. Do not invent exact peak masses or molecular structures; use precise white replacement slots for an illustrator if data are not supplied.
>
> **Panel b — MMA: PEAKS BECOME GRAPH-STRUCTURED CHEMICAL HYPOTHESES.** MMA must be visibly distinctive and roughly as prominent as G, not a footnote database symbol. Show ONE measured fragment peak from panel a, connected first to its possible ion-composition formula and then to TWO OR THREE alternative local fragment/motif graph placeholders. Annotate a small relative prior/support symbol. These are **candidate structures**, not directly observed fragment identities. Optionally place three subtle source candidates labelled “exact”, “H-shift” and “restricted edit”, if real source-backed motifs will replace the placeholders. Next show one candidate motif *aligned atom-to-atom and bond-to-bond* against the current partial precursor molecular graph, with exactly matching nodes/edges highlighted by a few thin dashed teal correspondence lines. Label two informative outputs: “peak-conditioned motif evidence” and “atom/bond coverage”. A thin line carries this graph-aligned chemical knowledge into G. The scientific caption reads: “From measured ions to reusable local structural hypotheses”.
>
> **Panel c — G: BUILD COMPLETE PRECURSOR GRAPHS.** For the SAME query, show 3–4 successive real 2D molecular states: initial small chemically valid partial graph; selected peak or goal; atom/ring/motif attachment under formula and valence constraints; complete candidate precursor molecules A and B. Label the action types sparingly: “select peak”, “grow”, “attach ring / motif”, “complete graph”. Show a tiny fine-lined strip “Peak-Ladder · Canonical Hybrid · atom/ring/motif beam search”. The **mstart / mattach** actions may carry a restrained highlight where an MMA-proposed multi-atom motif was added. The final structures A and B are formula-compatible but differ in specific local bonds/side-chain connectivity. This is a *generation* result and changes candidate reach; it does not claim top-1 identification. Molecular images must be composited from the actual source case using RDKit, not drawn from model imagination.
>
> **Panel d — W/V: CHEMICALLY TEST COMPETING PRECURSORS.** Visually separate forward structural hypotheses (W) from measured-data conditioning (V) within one scientific workspace. W independently takes candidate graph A or B from c and predicts plausible **structural fragment-ion subgraphs** and ion mass/formula slots for each. Mark “W: precursor-structure conditioned; observed target MS/MS excluded”. Never draw an observed experimental product-ion peak entering W. Beneath the W candidates, draw a real small **candidate × observed-ion peak evidence grid**: each row represents A or B, each column a real measured peak from a; use gray small squares for shared mass support, muted teal small squares for chemically discriminating evidence and hollow cells for unexplained peaks. The *actual measured spectrum* from a arrives at V on an independent thin routed line. W's predicted fragment candidates arrive on a different line. V combines them to produce an annotated “ranked structure” or “bounded isomer set” output; no invented confidence percentages, known GT output, or fake re-ranking. Short summary: “Candidate reach is not candidate discrimination”.
>
> **Panel e — SCIENTIFIC IMPROVER AGENT: TURN FAILED CHEMISTRY INTO LEARNING.** This is a lower-band scientific experiment loop, not an assistant chat screen. It starts with a **TRAIN FIT** diagnosis from previous WGV inference: “true structure absent from candidates”, “true structure present but misranked”, or “fragment evidence insufficient for a unique assignment”. One concrete failure is connected to an editable scientific hypothesis slip containing “observed chemical deficit”, “hypothesis about missing capability”, and “falsifiable prediction”. Then place a modest Scientific Improver Agent label choosing **exactly ONE** allowed intervention from: **expand MMA knowledge**, **improve G**, **improve V**, or **collect further TRAIN diagnostics**. Feed the chosen action to an execution/evaluation step using existing chemistry and training tools. The output is an evidence-backed before/after receipt recording “newly reached structures”, “corrected ranking”, “lost previous successes” and “compute cost if measured”; leave values empty until actual episodes finish. Show TWO visibly different return lines: a muted teal model/chemical-knowledge route updating next-generation MMA/G/V (W fixed in initial experiment), and a thin gray *reflection / outcome memory* route returning to the next Agent decision. Do NOT suggest that the Agent LLM weights changed. Do NOT represent proposed chemistry-specific target-cluster learning or cost-aware action choice as already implemented; they may be future additions discussed separately. Close with one small phrase: “Scientific aim: more structurally interpretable dark spectra”.
>
> **Critical figure logic:** experimental MS/MS reaches MMA/G and V; G candidates enter W; W output enters V; candidate evidence can change ranking and justified ambiguity; TRAIN FIT known-target failures are analyzed separately from unknown inference; an agent-selected training action can change future MMA/G/V capacity and produce reusable reflection memory. The figure must communicate *chemical objects*, *distinct causal problems*, *forward-vs-inverse evidence*, and *what recursive improvement actually updates*.
>
> **Data honesty and visual fidelity:** the generative render is an original **layout concept**, not source data. For absent inputs use well-sized blank slots labelled “measured MS/MS”, “source-derived RDKit motif”, “partial molecular graph”, “W fragment proposal”, “V evidence”, “episode receipt”. Do not hallucinate peaks, atoms, fragments, biological pathways, disjoint families, RSI gains, or source names. The final Figure 1 PDF/SVG must be reconstructed from original measured spectra, actual candidate SMILES, RDKit atomic graphs, W/V output receipts and (for a numeric RSI example) completed PR96 episodes.

## 6. Draft Figure 1 caption

**Fig. 1 | Evidence-guided precursor structure elucidation and recursive chemical learning in ORBIT-MS.** **a**, A measured tandem mass spectrum is compatible with multiple precursor molecular graphs. **b**, MMA converts observed fragment-ion mass information into candidate graph-structured motifs and aligns motif atom/bond evidence with a growing molecular graph. **c**, The Generator constructs legal complete precursor hypotheses using Peak-Ladder and Canonical Hybrid atom/ring/motif actions. **d**, The World predicts candidate-specific structural fragment hypotheses without the target experimental fragment peaks, whereas the Verifier uses the measured spectrum to rank or bound the competing precursor structures. **e**, Scientific Improver diagnoses labelled TRAIN FIT failures, selects one bounded chemical-knowledge or model-learning action, evaluates its consequences and records experience for subsequent decisions, enabling a proposed expansion of recoverable chemical structure.
